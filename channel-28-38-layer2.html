### Layer 2: The Player Who Chooses the Game

The Specific Pattern For You: Your INFP idealism sees SO MUCH that's wrong in the world. Injustice. Inauthenticity. Shallow priorities. Systems that harm people.

Your first instinct: Fight ALL of it. Fix everything. Stand up for everyone. Oppose every wrong.

Result: Complete burnout. Spread too thin. Fighting battles that aren't YOURS.

What's Actually Happening: Gate 28 asks BEFORE every struggle: "Is this WORTH it? Is this MY fight?"

Not "Is this important?" Not "Is this wrong?" Just: "Is this MY fight to fight?"

Your INFP wants to fight for ALL causes. But Channel 28-38 says: You have LIMITED fight-energy. Choose ONLY struggles that serve YOUR soul.

Your 3/5 + This Channel: Your 3 has LEARNED through trial-and-error: You've engaged in wrong struggles (people-pleasing, proving yourself, fighting someone else's battle) and paid the price in burnout.

Your 5 gets projected on: People SEE you as "the fighter" and expect you to champion their causes.

Together: People will PROJECT their struggles onto you. Gate 28 asks: "Is this mine to carry?"

If no → Put it down. Even if they're disappointed. Even if it looks "selfish." If yes → Your Sacral will say YES. Fight with full force.

The Cost You've Paid: Every time you fought a battle that wasn't YOURS: - You burned out - The struggle drained you instead of fueling you - You resented the people you were "helping" - You had no energy left for YOUR battles
