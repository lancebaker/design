The Timing Problem (Why You Feel Misunderstood)

The Specific Pattern For You

Gate 43 downloads insights FAST. Gate 23 wants to share them IMMEDIATELY. But your INFP needs the other person to UNDERSTAND deeply, not just hear you.

So you over-explain. You give context. You use metaphors. You try five different ways of saying it.

And the more you explain, the more confused they get. Because you're PUSHING an insight they're not ready to receive.

Your MG Strategy Saves You

"To Respond" means: WAIT to be ASKED before explaining your insights.

This feels counterintuitive. You have this brilliant insight - shouldn't you share it immediately?

No.

When you WAIT for an invitation ("What do you think about X?" "Can you explain that?" "I'm curious about..."), your insights land with POWER. The invitation creates the opening. Gate 23's timing aligns.

The Cost of Ignoring This

Every time you share unsolicited insights, two things happen: 1. People don't receive them (wrong timing) 2. You feel frustrated (not-self theme for MGs)

Over time, you start to believe "I'm bad at communication" or "people don't value my ideas."

False. You're a GENIUS at communication when you honor timing.
