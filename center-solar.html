Solar Plexus

Layer 1: Center Overview

The Pattern You've Lived

You walk into a room. The energy feels HEAVY. You think: "I feel terrible. Something's wrong with ME."

Or: You're with someone who's anxious. Suddenly YOU'RE anxious. You think: "Why am I suddenly anxious?"

Or: You're in a good mood. Someone upset enters. Your mood TANKS. You think: "What just happened?"

What's Actually Happening

UNDEFINED Solar Plexus means you DON'T have consistent emotional processing. You ABSORB and AMPLIFY others' emotions.

You're an emotional MIRROR. You reflect back what's in the environment, magnified.

The Insight That Changes Everything

Most of the "emotions" you feel AREN'T YOURS.

You're feeling: - Your partner's unexpressed anxiety - The collective mood of the room - The emotional residue in a space - The tension in someone's body language

You amplify it, experience it as YOUR emotion, then try to "fix" YOUR emotional problem.

But it's not YOURS to fix. It's not even YOURS.

How This Reframes Your Life

- Those times you felt "suddenly depressed" for no reason? You absorbed someone's emotion. - Your sensitivity to "vibes" in spaces? You're reading emotional fields others can't detect. - Your need for solitude to "recharge"? You're DISCHARGING absorbed emotions. The cave isn't avoidance - it's NECESSITY.

The Reframe

You're not "too sensitive" or "emotionally unstable." You're an emotional AMPLIFIER.

Your challenge: Discerning YOURS from THEIRS.

Layer 2: The Emotional Absorption Pattern

The Specific Pattern

You're alone, feeling FINE. Someone anxious calls you. 10 minutes later, YOU'RE anxious.

You think: "Why did I suddenly get anxious? What's wrong with me?"

Nothing's wrong with you. You ABSORBED their anxiety and amplified it.

Your INFP + Undefined Solar Plexus

Your Fe (extraverted feeling) makes you AWARE of others' emotional states.

Undefined Solar Plexus makes you ABSORB and AMPLIFY those states.

Together: You feel others' emotions more intensely than they feel them themselves.

The Challenge

You can't tell the difference between: - YOUR emotion (generated from your experience) - THEIR emotion (absorbed from their field)

Both FEEL like yours. Both feel REAL. But only one actually IS yours.

Your Split Definition

You have two separate definition areas. Your Solar Plexus is UNDEFINED and sits between them.

This means: You're unconsciously SEEKING emotional connection to bridge the split. But you can easily mistake absorbed emotions for "connection."

Layer 3: How This Shows Up in Your Life

In Relationships

You're with your partner. They're upset (but not expressing it). YOU start feeling upset.

You think: "Why am I upset? What's wrong?"

Nothing's wrong with YOU. You're FEELING their unexpressed emotion.

The Trap

You try to "fix" your upset (journaling, breathing, analyzing). But it's not YOURS, so nothing fixes it.

Then they LEAVE. Suddenly your upset vanishes. "Oh. That was THEIRS."

In Social Settings

You enter a party. Everyone's performing "having fun" but underneath: anxiety, competitiveness, performance.

You absorb ALL of it. You feel OVERWHELMED. You think: "I'm bad at socializing."

Not true. You're drowning in UNSPOKEN emotions that others aren't feeling because they're not amplifying them.

In Your Cave

After social time, you NEED solitude. Not because you're "introverted" (though your INFP is).

Because you need to DISCHARGE all the emotions you absorbed. Your nervous system needs to RESET to baseline.

This isn't optional. This is SURVIVAL for undefined Solar Plexus.

Your ADHD + Emotional Amplification

Emotional overwhelm DISRUPTS your ADHD regulation. When you're flooded with absorbed emotions, your executive function TANKS.

Then you think: "My ADHD is bad today."

Actually: You're emotionally flooded from absorption, which dysregulates your nervous system, which impacts ADHD.

The Fix

After emotional absorption (social time, conflict, being around others' stress): DISCHARGE before trying to work/create.

Walk. Music. Alone time. Physical movement. Let the absorbed emotions LEAVE your system.

THEN your ADHD regulation returns.

Layer 4: Living with Emotional Wisdom

What Full Integration Looks Like

Learn to DISCERN yours from theirs. Protect your emotional field. Honor your need to discharge.

Practically

1. The 24-Hour Rule

When you feel STRONG emotion, ask: "Is this mine, or did I absorb this?"

Wait 24 hours (or sleep on it). If the emotion is STILL present after time alone, it might be yours.

If it VANISHES after leaving the situation/person, it was absorbed.

2. The Body Check

Before entering emotional situations, notice your BASELINE: "How do I feel right now?"

After/during situation, check again: "Did this feeling START here? Or did I come in with it?"

If it started THERE, it's probably absorbed.

3. The Discharge Practice

After ANY emotional intensity (yours or absorbed): Physical discharge.
  • Shake your body
  • Dance
  • Walk in nature
  • Scream in your car
  • Heavy breathing

Let the energy MOVE through and OUT.

4. The Protection

You can't stop absorbing (it's your design). But you CAN choose environments.

Avoid: Spaces with heavy unspoken emotions (fake-happy work cultures, tense family dynamics, performative social scenes)

Seek: Spaces with CONGRUENT emotions (emotionally honest people, authentic spaces, nature)

Layer 5: Shadow & Breakthrough

The Shadow

Taking on responsibility for others' emotions as if they're YOURS to fix.

Someone's sad. You absorb their sadness. You think: "I need to cheer them up so I can feel better."

But you're not feeling better FOR you. You're feeling better to stop feeling THEIR emotion.

How This Shows Up

- Someone's upset. You try to fix it. Not because it's your responsibility, but because you're absorbing their upset and want it to STOP.
  • Your partner's anxious. You try to solve their problem. Not because they asked, but because you're feeling their anxiety and it's overwhelming.
  • Someone's disappointed in you. You contort yourself to make them happy. Not because you genuinely want to change, but because you're absorbing their disappointment and it's painful.

The Truth

Their emotions are NOT yours to fix. Even when you're feeling them.

The Fix

When you notice you're absorbing an emotion, ask: 1. "Is this mine?" 2. If no: "Can I let them have their feeling without absorbing it?" 3. If you can't: "Can I create space between us so I can discharge?"

You're not responsible for managing their emotions just because you're feeling them.

The Breakthrough

When you master discernment (yours vs. theirs):

1. Emotional overwhelm DECREASES - You stop taking on emotions that aren't yours 2. Relationships improve - You stop trying to "fix" people who don't need fixing 3. Your empathy becomes a GIFT - You can feel WITH people without drowning IN their emotions

The Proof

Times you felt most emotionally balanced? You were probably: - Spending time alone regularly (discharging) - With emotionally honest people (congruent fields) - In nature or calm spaces (low emotional noise)

THAT'S your baseline. Not the overwhelmed state.

The Reframe

You're not "emotionally unstable" or "too sensitive."

You're an EMOTIONAL AMPLIFIER. You feel others' emotions to help them feel FELT.

But you must protect your field, discharge regularly, and discern yours from theirs.

Solar Plexus undefined at its fullest: You feel others deeply WITHOUT losing yourself in their feelings. You offer empathy WITHOUT taking responsibility for their emotions. You honor your need for solitude as SACRED, not shameful.

Your emotional sensitivity is a GIFT. When you know how to work with it.
