The Head-Sacral Integration — Thinking vs. Knowing

Your Head and Sacral are in two different circuits — this is the Split Definition at work. Your Head asks questions. Your Sacral has answers. But they don't speak the same language, and they don't directly connect internally.

This creates a specific pattern: your Head generates a question ("Should I pursue this?"), then spins trying to think its way to clarity — but the answer was already in your Sacral the whole time. The Head can't access it directly. You have to drop the question from your mind into your body and notice the gut response BEFORE the mental analysis restarts.

Practice: when Gate 61 is generating an unanswerable existential loop, ask: "What does my Sacral say about the next concrete step?" Not the big question — just the next step. Your Sacral always has an answer for the immediate. Let that move you forward while your Head continues its beautiful, restless searching.
