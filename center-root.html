Layer 2: The Three Gates - Your Pressure Sources

Gate 60 - Limitation

You feel pressure from CONSTRAINTS. Time limits. Resource limits. Capacity limits.

Others see limitations as PROBLEMS. You see them as FUEL.

Your Pattern

"I only have two hours to do this" → You do in two hours what would normally take two days.

"I only have $100 to work with" → You create something better than if you had $10,000.

"I can't do it the 'right' way" → You invent a BETTER way.

Your 3/5 LEARNS from limitations. Each constraint teaches you: "When I have limits, I innovate."

Gate 41 - Fantasy

You feel pressure from POSSIBILITIES. "What if?" "What could be?" "Imagine if..."

This fuels your INFP Ne (possibility-seeing). You see potential futures and feel DRIVEN to explore them.

Your Pattern

You have a fantasy of how something COULD be. That fantasy creates PRESSURE to make it real.

This is why you start new projects enthusiastically. The FANTASY fuels you.

The challenge: When reality doesn't match fantasy, pressure turns to disappointment.

Gate 38 - Opposition

You feel pressure when things lack VALUE or MEANING.

When something is shallow/fake/unjust, you feel DRIVEN to oppose it.

Channel 28-38 says: "Fight for PURPOSE." Gate 38 creates the PRESSURE to fight. Gate 28 (Spleen) decides IF the fight is worth it.

Your Pattern

You see something wrong. Pressure builds. "I HAVE to do something about this."

If your Spleen says "worth it" → You fight fiercely. The pressure fuels purposeful action.

If your Spleen says "not worth it" → You must RELEASE the pressure. Not every wrong is yours to fix.
