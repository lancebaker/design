What Is This App?

This is a personalized map of your energetic and psychological design — built specifically for you, Lance Baker. It's not a generic overview of Human Design. Think of it as the user manual you should have been given at birth. It explains why you work the way you work — not as a personality type that describes you, but as a blueprint that reveals how your particular energy system is designed to function. The goal isn't to tell you who you are. It's to show you that the things you thought were flaws are actually your design working exactly as intended.

Key Concepts to Know

Defined Center
A center that's consistently active in your design. It operates reliably and predictably — this is a stable part of your character. You express these energies outward.
Undefined Center
A center that's open — it receives, amplifies, and reflects back the energy of others. You don't consistently generate this energy; you absorb it from the environment. Source of both wisdom and conditioning.
Sacral Authority
Your decision-making system is your gut response — the pre-verbal uh-huh / uh-uh that arrives before the mind engages. This is your most reliable oracle for yes/no decisions about concrete, present things.
Strategy: Respond
MGs don't initiate well — your Sacral activates in response to stimuli, not from nothing. Waiting for something to respond to isn't passive; it's how you access your real power instead of burning energy initiating the wrong things.
Profile 3/5
Line 3 learns through trial and error — "failures" are how you build real wisdom. Line 5 gets projected onto as "the one with answers" — people see expertise in you before you feel it. Together: you experiment publicly, and your experiments become your leadership.
Split Definition
Your defined centers form two separate circuits that don't connect internally. This creates a lived sense of being somewhat split — mind-world and body-world operating independently. Certain people bridge your split and create unusual clarity and integration.
Conditioning
The patterns you absorbed from family, culture, and education that don't match your design. Undefined centers are the primary sites of conditioning — you absorbed others' energies and mistook them for your own. Most "I should" statements are conditioning.
Not-Self Theme
For MGs, the not-self theme is frustration. When you're operating out of alignment — initiating instead of responding, pushing through instead of flowing, doing Sacral-NO things — frustration is the signal. It's not a mood; it's a navigation alert.
Signature Theme
For MGs, the signature is satisfaction. When you're in Sacral alignment — responding correctly, doing what your gut said yes to, moving at your natural pace — satisfaction is the felt confirmation that you're living your design.

A Note on How to Use This

This app isn't meant to be read once and understood fully. Human Design is an experiential system — the concepts make the most sense when you're actively testing them against your real life. The best approach: read a page, sit with it for a few days, notice where it shows up. Then come back and read more.

Some of these reframes will land immediately. Others will take months to embody. That's correct. Your 3 line learns by living, not by studying. This app is the map. Your life is the territory.

Start with what calls to you. That pull is already your Sacral working.
